Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EventAura handles information when you visit eventaura.space, send a booking enquiry, submit a review or contact us. EventAura provides event styling and decoration services in Lahore and Gujranwala.

1. Information you provide

If you submit a booking enquiry, we may receive the details you enter in the form, such as your name, phone number, event type, city, selected theme, event details and message. We use this information to respond to your enquiry, discuss availability and prepare an event proposal.

If you submit a review, we receive the name you provide, event type, rating and review text. Reviews are checked before publication. If approved, the review and the name you supplied may be displayed on this website. Please do not include private contact details or sensitive information in a public review.

2. How enquiries and reviews are handled

Booking enquiry forms are submitted through FormSubmit, which processes the form information and forwards it to EventAura's support email. Review submissions and published-review data are handled through a Google Apps Script and associated Google services. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

You may also contact us through WhatsApp, Instagram or email. Those services process information you choose to send through them.

3. Analytics and website technologies

EventAura uses Google Analytics 4 to understand website visits and interactions. The current measurement ID configured on the website is G-ZVWVGTRBHB. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and process information such as pages viewed, device and browser details, approximate location inferred from network information, and interactions with the website.

The website may store your light or dark display preference in your browser's local storage. This preference is used to remember your chosen appearance.

4. Advertising and cookies

Google AdSense code is installed on this website. If ads are served, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to deliver, measure, limit or personalise advertising where permitted. Third parties may place and read cookies on your browser, or use web beacons or IP addresses, as a result of ad serving.

Where required for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, advertising consent is handled through the consent options configured for the publisher account. You can manage available advertising preferences through Google Ads Settings. For more information, read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Third-party resources and links

The website may load fonts, images, scripts or other resources from third-party providers. When your browser requests those resources, the providers may receive ordinary technical connection information such as your IP address and browser details. Links to WhatsApp, Instagram and other external websites are governed by those providers' own privacy policies.

6. Data security and retention

We use the information received to respond to enquiries, coordinate event discussions, review feedback and operate the website. We take reasonable care with information but cannot guarantee the security of information transmitted over the internet or processed by independent third-party services. Do not send payment card details, passwords, identity documents or other highly sensitive information through website forms or public reviews.

7. Your choices and contact

You may choose not to submit a form and may contact us directly instead. For a privacy question or request about information you have sent to EventAura, email support@eventaura.space.
